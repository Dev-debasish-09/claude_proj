Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

01

Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly we store the expenses you enter, including the amount, category, date and any description you add. We do not collect payment card details or bank credentials.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to build the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
